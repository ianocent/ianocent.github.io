Managing the Digital Presence of Suzuki Gading Serpong

Dwi Rianto 4 min read August 2026

Suzuki Gading Serpong is an official Suzuki dealer network in Tangerang — part of PT. Buana Indomobil Trada. I manage its digital presence across multiple sales handles: Vira (vira-suzukigadingserpong.com), Kiki (suzukibuanaindomobiltrada.com), Cinta (dealersuzukigadingserpong.com), and Singgih (showroommobilsuzukigadingserpong.com) — their content, and their search visibility.

This case study covers how the sites are built, what I maintain day-to-day, and how local search traffic for Suzuki Gading Serpong keywords is growing — no WordPress, no templates, fully hand-coded.

Tech Stack

Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS Boxicons Google Fonts MixItUp Schema.org AutoDealer Google Maps Embed WhatsApp Deep Links GitHub Pages

What I Manage

1. Promo & Unit Pages

The core of the site is the promo catalog — Ertiga Hybrid, XL7, Jimny, Grand Vitara, Fronx, and New Carry Pick Up — each with its own offer card: DP ringan, cicilan ringan, diskon, bonus aksesoris, and tukar tambah.

2. WhatsApp Sales Integration

Every unit card links straight to WhatsApp with a pre-filled message per model — so a visitor can ask about a Jimny or an XL-7 without retyping anything. Each handle routes to its own sales representative — Vira Shalim, Kiki Wulandari, Cinta (Salsa), and Singgih Priambodo — who receives the enquiry with full context: which unit, which promo, which handle.

3. Simulasi Kredit

A dedicated simulasi kredit page lets visitors estimate DP and monthly installments before contacting the dealer — reducing friction for credit buyers, which is most of the local market.

4. Local SEO & Structured Data

Each handle site ships with Schema.org AutoDealer markup — address, geo coordinates, opening hours, area served (Tangerang, BSD, Gading Serpong, Alam Sutera), and phone numbers. Combined with HTML sitemaps, Google Maps embeds, and geo meta tags, the sites are strongly mapped to the dealer suzuki gading serpong and suzuki tangerang search intents — Vira and Singgih anchor the dealer-name and showroom queries, Cinta extends coverage into Serpong/Tangsel, and Kiki ties the dealer brand to the Buana Indomobil Trada name.

5. Gallery & Social Proof

Delivery photos of real units (Jimny, XL-7, Fronx, Carry) build trust and double as long-tail image-search entry points via descriptive alt text and an image sitemap.

Performance & Hosting

Static-first, zero-framework pages served from a CDN-backed host with preconnect hints — the pages are lightweight, LCP stays low, and the promo popup is a single lazy-loaded image. No build step means edits ship in seconds.

Why This Setup Wins for Local Search

  • Keyword-matched structure — promo pages, contact, gallery, and simulasi kredit each target one intent cluster around suzuki gading serpong.
  • Real business signals — physical address, Google Maps, opening hours, and consistent NAP across the page.
  • Direct conversion path — every visit can convert to a WhatsApp chat in one tap; traffic quality matters as much as volume.
  • Cross-site authority — all four handle sites linked from my portfolio and blog, interlinked with each other, with rel="me" ownership signals tying the sites together.
